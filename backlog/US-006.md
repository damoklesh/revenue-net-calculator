# US-006 — Calculate French withholding and annual tax projections

## User Story
As an employee in France, I want to apply my known withholding rate or project annual income tax so that I can estimate net salary after tax.

## Context
US-005 provides employee contributions and payroll taxable net N. The projection supports one fiscal share and the initial single-person profile only. The 2026 assessment scale applies to 2025 income; using it with a 2026 salary is a labelled projection, not a definitive 2026-income tax assessment.

## Scope
- In scope: Mutually exclusive personal-PAS and annual-IR projection modes, marginal tax calculation, employment deduction and decote.
- Out of scope: Actual tax notices, definitive 2026-income liability, household shares, tax credits and special deductions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: The user chooses either a known PAS rate or a single-profile annual projection; both deductions are never applied together.
- [ ] AC-2: The known PAS rate accepts 0–100% and applies to payroll taxable net N, not gross salary or cash net.
- [ ] AC-3: Annual projection applies the employment deduction, marginal brackets and decote stated in Technical Notes; unsupported automatic profiles require manual mode.
- [ ] AC-4: The result clearly states that the 2026 scale for 2025 income is used as a projection.
- [ ] AC-5: The annual summary reconciles gross, contributions, the selected tax deduction and net, and explains withholding versus projected liability.

- [ ] AC-6: PAS and projected-IR results expose distinct machine-readable tax-mode identifiers and warnings suitable for US-009 rendering.
- [ ] AC-7: Switching tax mode recomputes from the same contribution result without mutating or double-counting the contribution lines.

## Technical Notes
- Preserve the existing stack and conventions where present.
- Known-rate mode: PAS=N×rate/100; annual cash net=G-C-PAS.
- Projection: D=min(N,max(509,min(0.10×N,14555))); B=max(0,N-D). Apply continuous marginal brackets: up to 11600 at 0%; to 29579 at 11%; to 84577 at 30%; to 181917 at 41%; excess at 45%.
- Iraw is the bracket sum; decote=max(0,897-0.4525×Iraw); estimated tax=max(0,Iraw-decote), using documented fiscal rounding; projected annual net=G-C-estimated tax.
- FR-01 with PAS 10%: N=29523.24, PAS=2952.32, annual net=25545.19. For a separate taxable-base fixture B=30000, Iraw=2103.99 before decote.
- References: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1419 ; https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1989 ; https://www.economie.gouv.fr/particuliers/impots-et-fiscalite/gerer-mon-impot-sur-le-revenu/pouvez-vous-beneficier-de-la-decote-de-limpot-sur-le-revenu . - Implementation plan:
  1. Extend France calculation input with mutually exclusive tax modes: known PAS rate and automatic single-profile projection.
  2. In PAS mode, validate rate `[0,100]`, apply it to payroll taxable net `N`, round according to the shared fiscal/money policy and set projected annual tax to null.
  3. In projection mode, compute the employment deduction `D`, taxable base `B`, marginal bracket tax `Iraw`, decote and estimated tax in the documented order; set withholding to null.
  4. Keep bracket parameters and deduction/decote constants in the France versioned ruleset rather than inside UI code.
  5. Reject unsupported automatic profiles with a typed unsupported-profile result that directs the UI toward manual PAS mode.
  6. Produce a single France final result where the selected tax amount is subtracted exactly once from `G-C`; include tax-mode id, projection warning and ruleset metadata.
  7. Add tax deduction lines distinct from contribution lines so US-009 can label/reconcile them without formula knowledge.
- Add pure helper tests for employment deduction, progressive brackets, decote and final-mode orchestration separately; avoid one monolithic opaque calculator test.
- Preserve the distinction between withholding cash flow and projected annual liability in field names and warnings.
