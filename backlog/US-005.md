# US-005 — Calculate French employee contributions

## User Story
As an employee in France, I want employee contributions deducted from gross salary so that I can estimate my net salary before income tax.

## Context
Use the 2026 general private-sector regime outside Alsace-Moselle, with cadre/non-cadre status and the original fixed-salary assumptions. Employer supplementary health/protection contributions, overtime, benefits in kind and special collective-agreement rates are excluded. French contribution reference: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2302 ; supplementary pension: https://reglementation.agirc-arrco.fr/home/baremes/listes-area/baremes-1/cotisations-au-regime-agirc-arrco-en-2026.html . Reference snapshot: 2026-09-30.

## Scope
- In scope: French employee contribution bases, rates, ceilings, cadre differences and payroll taxable net.
- Out of scope: Employer contributions, supplementary insurance, income tax, household calculations and unsupported regimes.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: The engine implements all contribution bases, ceilings and rates specified in Technical Notes.
- [ ] AC-2: The result distinguishes cash net before tax from payroll taxable net and returns each contribution's base, rate and amount.
- [ ] AC-3: CET activates only when G exceeds P and applies to both T1 and T2; APEC applies only to cadres and is capped at 4P.
- [ ] AC-4: The result lists omitted supplementary insurance and special agreement effects as assumptions.
- [ ] AC-5: Unsupported regimes produce an explicit unsupported-profile result rather than a generic percentage deduction.

- [ ] AC-6: Every returned French contribution line has a stable concept identifier in addition to display-independent numeric base/rate/amount data.
- [ ] AC-7: The France adapter consumes versioned rule parameters rather than embedding rates directly in presentation or orchestration code.

## Technical Notes
- Preserve the existing stack and conventions where present.
- For annual gross G, use annual ceiling P = EUR 48,060 (monthly ceiling EUR 4,005). T1 = min(G,P), T2 = min(max(G-P,0),7P).
- Employee rates: uncapped old-age 0.40% of G; capped old-age 6.90% of T1; Agirc-Arrco 3.15% of T1 and 8.64% of T2; CEG 0.86% of T1 and 1.08% of T2.
- If G > P, CET is 0.14% of T1 + T2. For cadres only, APEC is 0.024% of min(G,4P).
- Under the excluded-employer-supplement assumptions, CSG/CRDS base A = 0.9825 × min(G,4P) + max(G-4P,0). Deductible CSG = 6.80% of A; nondeductible CSG = 2.40% of A; CRDS = 0.50% of A.
- C is the sum of employee contributions. Net before tax = G-C. Payroll taxable net N = G-C+nondeductible CSG+CRDS. Do not add an ordinary employee unemployment-insurance deduction.
- FR-01 fixture: non-cadre G=36000, no supplemental insurance: C=7502.49, net before tax=28497.51, N=29523.24. Round each annual contribution line half-up to cents and sum the rounded lines.


- Implementation plan:
  1. Add a versioned France 2026 ruleset containing ceiling `P`, contribution rates and descriptive source metadata from this story.
  2. Implement a pure France contribution function that receives normalized annual gross and cadre status and computes `T1`, `T2` and the CSG/CRDS base before line amounts.
  3. Compute each employee contribution as a separate line with stable id, base, rate and half-up cent-rounded annual amount.
  4. Apply CET only when `G > P`; apply it to `T1 + T2`. Apply APEC only for cadres and cap its base at `4P`.
  5. Compute `C` as the sum of rounded employee contribution lines, then derive cash net before tax and payroll taxable net `N` using nondeductible CSG and CRDS exactly once.
  6. Short-circuit zero gross before minimum/base logic so every contribution and derived amount is zero.
  7. Add assumptions/warnings for excluded supplementary insurance, Alsace-Moselle and special-agreement effects as structured result metadata.
  8. Return through the common `SalaryResult`/France intermediate contract expected by US-006; do not render labels in React here.
- Keep rates in ruleset data rather than local component/constants files. Tests should instantiate the adapter with the explicit France ruleset version.
- Use the FR-01 fixture as a golden test; if computed values differ, investigate formula/rounding order rather than changing the fixture silently.
