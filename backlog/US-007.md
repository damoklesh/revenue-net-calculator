# US-007 — Calculate Spanish employee contributions

## User Story
As an employee in Spain, I want my employee contributions applied so that I can estimate my net salary before IRPF withholding.

## Context
Support the 2026 General Regime, monthly groups 1–7, full-year constant monetary salary and ordinary indefinite/fixed-term contracts. Daily groups, part-time and special regimes are excluded. Spanish contribution reference: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7296 , articles 3, 4, 16, 17 and 33. Reference snapshot: 2026-09-30.

## Scope
- In scope: Monthly contribution bases, group minimums, maximum base, employee contributions and solidarity bands.
- Out of scope: Employer costs, special schemes, daily groups, part-time employment and IRPF withholding.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: The calculation applies the Technical Notes rates according to contract type and the selected monthly group.
- [ ] AC-2: The monthly contribution base includes prorated extra payments and respects the group's minimum and EUR 5,101.20 maximum.
- [ ] AC-3: Solidarity applies to remuneration exceeding the ceiling independently of the capped ordinary contribution base.
- [ ] AC-4: Employer contributions never reduce the employee's net; unsupported profiles receive a clear explanation.
- [ ] AC-5: Positive salaries below a contribution minimum use that minimum and display a theoretical-simulation warning; zero gross returns zero contributions.

- [ ] AC-6: Each Spanish employee contribution and solidarity component is emitted as a stable deduction line with its base, rate and amount.
- [ ] AC-7: Contribution-group minimums, ceiling and solidarity bands come from the versioned Spain ruleset rather than UI constants.

## Technical Notes
- Preserve the existing stack and conventions where present.
- Let G be annual gross and M=G/12 including prorated extra payments. BC=min(max(M,groupMinimum),5101.20); zero gross short-circuits all bases to zero.
- Monthly minimums: group 1=1989.30; 2=1649.70; 3=1435.20; groups 4–7=1424.40. Only monthly contribution groups are supported.
- Employee rates on BC: common contingencies 4.70%; unemployment 1.55% for ordinary indefinite contracts or 1.60% for ordinary fixed-term contracts; training 0.10%; MEI 0.15%.
- Additional employee solidarity on M above 5101.20: 0.19% on the band up to 5611.32, 0.21% on the band up to 7651.80, 0.24% on excess. Apply continuous marginal bands without cent-sized gaps.
- Annual contributions C=12×monthly ordinary contributions plus 12×monthly solidarity, with documented annual-line cent rounding. Do not deduct employer accident-insurance or FOGASA contributions.
- ES-01: G=36000, group 7, indefinite; common=1692, unemployment=558, training=36, MEI=54, C=2340; net before IRPF=33660.
- ES-02: same inputs with an ordinary fixed-term contract; unemployment=576, C=2358. These rates do not represent special fixed-term categories.


- Implementation plan:
  1. Add a versioned Spain 2026 contribution ruleset with monthly group minimums, maximum contribution base, contract-specific unemployment rates, MEI and solidarity bands.
  2. Derive monthly remuneration `M=G/12`; zero gross short-circuits before applying minimum bases.
  3. Compute ordinary monthly base `BC=min(max(M, groupMinimum), 5101.20)` for supported groups 1-7 only.
  4. Emit separate monthly/annual lines for common contingencies, unemployment, training and MEI, using the selected ordinary indefinite/fixed-term rate.
  5. Compute solidarity independently from the capped ordinary base using continuous marginal bands above 5101.20, then annualize and round using the shared policy.
  6. Sum only employee deductions into `C`; never include employer accident insurance or FOGASA.
  7. For positive salary below a group minimum, return a theoretical-simulation warning; for daily/part-time/special regimes return typed unsupported-profile information.
  8. Return a Spain intermediate/result shape suitable for US-008 with contribution lines and rule provenance.
- Keep group/rate/band data out of React and out of tests except fixture expectations; tests should load the real versioned ruleset.
