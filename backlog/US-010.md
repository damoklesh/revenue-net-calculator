# US-010 — Show twelve or fourteen salary payments in Spain

## User Story
As an employee with extra salary payments, I want ordinary and extra payments shown separately so that I can understand my income schedule.

## Context
Spain's annual gross remains the canonical input. Social contributions already include prorated extra payments in G/12. This version assumes equal gross amounts per payment and constant withholding; France remains at 12 payments.

Mockup references:
- `docs/mockups/MK-003-calculator-input-form.png`
- `docs/mockups/MK-005-spain-12-vs-14-payments.png`
Use the Spain-specific payments mockup as the primary reference for the 12/14-payment choice, explanatory help and result presentation.
Implementation note: Acceptance Criteria take precedence over the mockup if a conflict exists.

Dependency contract: US-009 provides the result presentation and current valid result state; US-003 provides canonical annual gross and input editing rules. Extend Spain payment scheduling without changing France or annual fiscal totals.

Use `MK-003` for placement of the payment-count control inside the calculator flow and `MK-005` for the 12-versus-14 explanation and payment-result presentation. Do not treat the mockup's sample numbers as test fixtures.

## Scope
- In scope: Spanish 12/14-payment selector, period conversion, payment breakdown and cent reconciliation.
- Out of scope: Unequal contractual payments, French 13th-month pay, mid-year changes and withholding regularization.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: Spain supports 12 or 14 equal-gross payments; France keeps 12.
- [ ] AC-2: For fixed G, changing payment count preserves contribution base G/12, annual contributions and annual withholding.
- [ ] AC-3: With 14 payments, contributions are deducted across 12 ordinary payments; the two extras do not repeat already prorated contribution deductions.
- [ ] AC-4: Withholding applies to gross per payment, and final-payment rounding residues reconcile the annual total.
- [ ] AC-5: Monthly input means gross per payment; changing payment count preserves canonical G and updates the displayed gross per payment.

- [ ] AC-6: The payment-count control and Spain-specific explanation are consistent with `MK-003` and `MK-005` and are hidden/disabled for France as appropriate.
- [ ] AC-7: The payment schedule exposes explicit ordinary/extra payment types and sums exactly to annual net after final-cent reconciliation.

## Technical Notes
- Preserve the existing stack and conventions where present.
- For 14 payments, ordinary net=G/14-withholdingPerPayment-C/12 and extra net=G/14-withholdingPerPayment, with an explicit residual policy.
- ES-01: G=36000, C=2340, manual rate=15%; annual net=28260. Approximate ordinary net=1990.71 and extra net=2185.71, with final-cent adjustment.
- Represent payment types explicitly; monthly average net remains annualNet/12 and is distinct from net per payment.


- Implementation plan:
  1. Extend Spain form state with `paymentsPerYear: 12 | 14`; France remains fixed at 12 and should not expose an editable selector.
  2. Preserve `annualGross` as canonical. When displaying monthly/per-payment gross, derive it from canonical annual gross and payment count; do not repeatedly multiply/divide already rounded display values.
  3. Extend `paymentSchedule` entries with explicit `ordinary`/`extra` type, sequence number, gross, contribution deduction, withholding and net.
  4. For 12 payments, distribute annual values consistently with existing monthly behaviour and reconcile the last payment.
  5. For 14 payments, deduct `C/12` only from 12 ordinary payments; extras receive no duplicated contribution deduction. Apply withholding to each payment gross according to the selected annual withholding model and reconcile residue on the final scheduled payment.
  6. Keep `monthlyAverageNet = annualNet/12` as an analytical average distinct from any ordinary or extra payment net.
  7. Update calculator UI using `MK-003` placement and add the explanation/comparison treatment from `MK-005` for Spain.
  8. Switching 12 ↔ 14 invalidates the current result, preserves canonical annual gross and other compatible Spain inputs, then recalculates only on user action.
- Do not alter Spain annual contribution formulas in US-007 or annual withholding totals in US-008 merely to make per-payment display easier.
